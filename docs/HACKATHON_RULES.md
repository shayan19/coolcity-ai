# Hackathon Rules

CoolCity AI is a United States urban cooling policy-intelligence product for FortyGuard Hackathon '26.

- FortyGuard remains the primary observed-temperature source and is integrated server-side.
- Provider credentials never enter browser code, fixtures, logs, reports, or committed files.
- Automated verification consumes no provider credits.
- Live requests require an intentional user action and the server-side safety gate.
- Supporting land-cover data never replace or modify a FortyGuard observation.
- Intervention effects use verified US air-temperature evidence, explicit uncertainty, and bounded local calibration only when quality gates pass.
- No fabricated training rows, forced targets, guaranteed cooling, exact cooling, surface-to-air substitution, or avoided-emissions claims are allowed.
- Shade, solar, quantitative water, Open-Meteo, and unused OSM building systems remain intentionally removed unless a later explicit product decision reintroduces them.
